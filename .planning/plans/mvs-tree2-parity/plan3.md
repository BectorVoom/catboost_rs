both `boost_from_average` settings** for MVS, generated by a NEW targeted

- **`git stash` on `crates/cb-train/src/bootstrap.rs` is forbidden** (CRITICAL-3).
- **SPEC `R3` — a shared border set.** Each scenario loads its own

- **Fixture size creep.** Ten scenarios is the spec's floor ("≥5 seeds × both bias
