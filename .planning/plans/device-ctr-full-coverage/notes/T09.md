## 5. Deviations from the task text

1. **A second test, `buckets_keeps_the_borders_shape_checks`.** The task names one test but
   states the goal as "declines every other discriminant **with the shape checks otherwise
   unchanged (C-7)**". The admission test alone cannot see a per-type shape special case, so
   C-7 would have shipped unpinned. The extra test is 40 lines, has its own §2.5 mutation
   (§3.2), and costs nothing at runtime.

2. **The negative loop covers all four unimplemented `ECtrType` discriminants plus four
   out-of-enum strays**, not just `ctr_type = 4`. This is what makes the conjunct provably an
   admission *list* rather than e.g. `col.ctr_type <= 1` (which would pass the task's literal
   Red but admit `-1`). `matches!` on the two `pub(crate)` consts is the shipped form.

3. **Both `.all(..)` closures were reflowed from expression to block form.** Required to carry
   the C-3/C-7 comment the plan mandates on an inline transcription. The two shape conjuncts
   are character-identical inside.

4. **The `# Errors` note for `CbError::Unsupported` was added to `build_ctr_cindex_columns`'s
   own (GDC-09) doc comment, not to the large `/// Compute the ADDITIONAL binarized-CTR cindex
   columns …` block above it** — because that block is **misattached** (see §6). Moving it was
   judged out of scope for T09.

Nothing else changed. The `cb-train` gate `ctr_types_are_device_covered` was **not** touched
(T10 owns it), the border-table shape check was **not** touched (C-7), and `ctr_covered`
was **not** widened to `pub(crate)` (C-11).
