## 5. ⚠ R-20 STATUS: **OPEN.** T18 did not close it and could not have.

Stated plainly, because this is the one claim in this task it would be easy to overstate.

**What is proved.**

1. **The helper filters.** `eligible_max_excludes_ineligible_combinations` is red-first
   (`left: 40, right: 6`) and every conjunct of the filter is individually mutation-proved
   (MUT-1, MUT-2, MUT-6, §3).
2. **The composition is C-16-shaped.** `phantom_max_is_folded_in_outside_the_eligibility_filter`

3. **The call site exists and has the C-16 shape** — `pass_c_calls_the_filtered_max_and_folds_the_phantom_outside_it`,
   a **textual** scan of `include_str!("mod.rs")`, mutation-proved by MUT-4 (un-wire ⇒ red) and
   MUT-5 (route the phantom through the filter ⇒ red).
4. **The helper's return value is consumed by the production cat-feature weight** — MUT-W moves
   `device_ctr_fit_test` to `|Δ| = 1.188e-1`.

**What is NOT proved, and why no test in this task could.**

While the cb-train gate still carries its projection-arity conjunct (**T19** removes it, not
T18), every CTR column that reaches pass C has **exactly one member**, so
`resident_combination_eligible` is unconditionally `true` and **the filter is the identity on
every reachable production input**. There is therefore no fit — none, on any committed fixture —
whose output can differ between filtered and unfiltered `eligible_max`. That is not a gap in the
fixtures; it is a property of the current gate. `SPEC.md` DCTR-16 and R-20 say exactly this:
`ctr_device_combo` does **not** discriminate D-2 (D-1 alone already passes it at `2.082e-17`).

**Does the source scan "prove wiring"?** Partly, and only in a way worth stating precisely:

* it proves, at the **source text** level, that pass C computes `eligible_max` by calling the
  filtered helper and folds the phantom in outside it;
* combined with MUT-W it proves the value that call produces is **consumed** by the weight;
* it does **not** prove that *filtering* changes any observable outcome, and it never can —
  it asserts about characters in a file, not about a fit;
* it would miss any wiring defect that preserves the call text: a wrong argument (e.g. a
  `projection_members` list that is empty, stale, or misaligned with `bucket_counts` — T17's
  MUT-3a/3b class), a `chosen_ctr_projections` that is populated wrongly, or a second unfiltered
  `eligible_max` computed elsewhere and used instead;
* it is deliberately fail-loud on rename/reformat, which is a maintenance cost a later task may
  choose to pay differently.

**Therefore: R-20 stays OPEN, exactly as PLAN §6 assumption 6 and `SPEC.md` R-20 record it.**
The primary evidence is **T22's device-vs-CPU split-sequence differential (DCTR-20)**, and
whether that differential actually discriminates D-2 is **UNMEASURED as of T18**. T22's
